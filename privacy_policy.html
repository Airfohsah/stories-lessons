Privacy Policy

App: Stories & Lessons
Last updated: 20 November 2026

Stories & Lessons ("the App", "we") is a small publishing tool used by its owner to schedule and post the owner's own original story videos to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Who uses the App

The App is operated by its owner for personal publishing. It is not offered to the public and does not collect information from visitors or other TikTok users.

2. Information we access

When the owner connects their TikTok account through TikTok Login, the App receives from TikTok:

  • basic profile information (such as display name and avatar), through the user.info.basic permission;
  • an access token and a refresh token that allow the App to upload and publish videos on the owner's behalf, through the video.upload and video.publish permissions.

The App does not access private messages, contacts, follower lists, or any information about other TikTok users.

3. How we use information

Information is used only to sign in to the connected TikTok account, upload the owner's own videos, publish them at the times the owner chooses, and check whether each post succeeded.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on the owner's own computer or private server and are not shared with anyone. We take reasonable steps to keep them secure.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties. Information is exchanged only with TikTok, as needed to perform the actions described above.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. The owner can disconnect at any time by removing the App's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services), and can delete the stored tokens from the computer where they are kept. After access is removed, the App can no longer act on the account.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
